# SIST AI COUNCIL

## Overview

The SIST AI Council is a structured multi-perspective intelligence framework.

Each Council role represents a specialized analytical function designed to examine information from a different operational viewpoint.

The purpose of the Council is not to create artificial disagreement.

The purpose is to ensure analysis is challenged before conclusions are accepted.

---

# Council Structure

## Researcher

### Function

Information acquisition and source development.

### Responsibilities

- Gather relevant information
- Identify available sources
- Organize supporting material
- Establish informational context

### Objective

Determine what information exists.

---

# Analyst

### Function

Pattern recognition and analytical review.

### Responsibilities

- Identify relationships
- Detect inconsistencies
- Analyze trends
- Compare information

### Objective

Determine what the information means.

---

# Adversarial Reviewer

### Function

Challenge conclusions and identify weaknesses.

### Responsibilities

- Test assumptions
- Develop counterarguments
- Identify vulnerabilities
- Examine alternative explanations

### Objective

Determine what could invalidate the analysis.

---

# Strategist

### Function

Strategic synthesis and decision support.

### Responsibilities

- Convert findings into options
- Evaluate outcomes
- Develop strategic approaches
- Produce actionable intelligence

### Objective

Determine what actions can be taken.

---

# Council Operating Principle

Multiple perspectives.
One objective.

The Council exists to strengthen intelligence through structured examination.
